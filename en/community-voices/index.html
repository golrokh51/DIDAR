COMMUNITY VOICES · SINCE 2017

Stories of Support & Its Impact

How support from people now on the DIDAR team has shaped individual journeys.

Since 2017, individuals who are now members of DIDAR’s team have provided informal, voluntary support to other people. Here, those who received that support share what they were navigating, what help they received, and what difference it made to them.

These experiences may predate DIDAR’s formal establishment. They are personal accounts of help offered by one or more current team members, not testimonials from participants in DIDAR programs or proof of formally delivered services.

People’s experiences, in their own terms

Each account is an edited, anonymized summary of a person’s experience, published only after that person has reviewed and approved the exact wording. Reported changes are based on their own assessment.

Respecting people’s stories

No names, identifying details, sensitive personal information, or unapproved drafts are published here. A person may request a correction or removal. Any translations are published only after approval.
