      What DIDAR can learn

      Early responses underscore the role of trusted connections, emotional encouragement and practical information. The reported needs suggest areas to explore through future collaboration, such as employment guidance, social connection and navigation toward existing services. DIDAR does not replace qualified legal, immigration, medical or psychological professionals.
