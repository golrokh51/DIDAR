Support goes both ways

You support DIDAR. We help people discover your work.

DIDAR wants to acknowledge its supporters and help community members discover their creative work, professional skills, and services. Everyone receives respectful recognition—without ranking people by the size of their contribution.

Contact us about collaborating

What could your profile include?

  • Your chosen public name and a short self-written or approved bio.
  • An artwork image, logo, or avatar that you have permission to share.
  • A description of your contribution and a link to your website or portfolio.
  • No requirement to share your age, legal name, or donation amount.

Participation is voluntary. Profiles can be updated or removed upon request. Being featured is a thank-you, not a commercial endorsement or certification.

This page may recognize individuals, informal groups, associations and businesses. Publication requires permission, and listing a supporter does not imply endorsement of its products, views or services.
