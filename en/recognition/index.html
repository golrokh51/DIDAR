Discover Montréal-area businesses and professionals selected by DIDAR for recognition and community visibility. These are independent businesses, not DIDAR programs or service providers.

Behind many newcomer journeys are businesses and professionals who have made a meaningful difference. Over the years, local businesses and individuals have supported members of our community in many ways: by sharing valuable information, offering guidance, creating employment opportunities, treating newcomers with fairness and respect, or simply extending a helping hand when it was needed. Through this page, DIDAR wishes to recognize and celebrate these acts of kindness, solidarity and social responsibility. Our recognition is inspired by the personal experiences of DIDAR team members and others in our community. We believe that meaningful community support often begins with small, everyday actions—and that these contributions deserve to be acknowledged.

DIDAR chooses which businesses and professionals to feature and contacts them directly. A listing recognizes their connection to DIDAR; it is not a certification, recommendation, or guarantee of their products or services.
