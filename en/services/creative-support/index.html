Services & Learning

Ways DIDAR can help

We discuss each request to identify a realistic, accessible form of community support.

01

Share an exhibition

Help circulate an exhibition, opening, or art event announcement through DIDAR channels when possible.

02

Introduce an emerging artist

Consider a short profile, interview, or feature about your practice, work, and artistic journey.

03

Spotlight a small creative venture

Introduce handmade projects, creative Instagram shops, and small independent initiatives.

04

Build connections

Facilitate introductions to other artists, volunteers, partners, or spaces where opportunities arise.

05

Share digital know-how

Offer basic guidance on portfolio links, promotional visuals, online presence, and sharing event information.

✳

Support, not a guarantee. Features, volunteer capacity, and assistance are considered case by case. DIDAR does not guarantee an exhibition venue, sales, funding, or professional advertising services.

Who is this for?

How to propose your project

You can get in touch even if your creative work is just getting started.

  1. 01

    Introduce yourself

    Your name or artist name, creative field, and city.

  2. 02

    Share your project

    A link to your work, Instagram page, or portfolio; event dates if relevant.

  3. 03

    Tell us what support would help

    Exhibition promotion, a website feature, community introductions, or basic digital guidance.

We will not publish your name, images, contact details, or personal story without your explicit permission. You retain ownership of your work and images.
