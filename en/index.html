DIDAR — A cultural, educational & community space in Montréal

A place to learn together, share perspectives and build lasting connections.

DIDAR brings people together through learning, cultural activities and practical community support. We create welcoming spaces to participate, exchange knowledge and find your way to useful resources—without replacing professional services.

دیدار

What does DIDAR mean?

In Persian, didâr evokes meeting, reconnecting and coming together. It reflects the relationships at the heart of what we do.

About DIDAR

Community begins with connection.

DIDAR is a Montréal-based cultural, educational and community initiative. We bring people together through learning, arts, exchange and practical mutual support.

How our story began

What started with people helping one another navigate unfamiliar situations grew into a wider network of shared knowledge, mentoring and accompaniment. Our detailed story preserves that journey.

Who is DIDAR for?

Everyone who wants to learn, connect or contribute is welcome. Some activities are designed for particular communities or needs.

Navigation & support

Need a starting point?

We can help you identify resources, prepare questions or find people and organizations to contact. Practical accompaniment depends on volunteer availability.

Ask for guidance →

Our role is community connection, information, referrals and possible accompaniment. We do not replace regulated legal, immigration, medical, counselling or social-work services.
