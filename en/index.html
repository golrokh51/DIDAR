Why DIDAR?

From community support to a shared structure.

The idea behind DIDAR grew from years of informal community support in Montréal. Again and again, people were facing similar challenges: not necessarily because resources did not exist, but because they did not know where to go, how unfamiliar systems worked, whom to trust, or how to ask for help.

Over time, individual acts of support became networks of mentoring, referrals, practical accompaniment, mutual aid, cultural activities and community connection. DIDAR is an effort to give that experience a more durable, collective and accessible structure.

About DIDAR

A cultural, educational and community initiative built around connection.

DIDAR develops initiatives that strengthen autonomy, inclusion, learning, social connection and participation. We help people identify resources, prepare next steps, connect with appropriate organizations and professionals, and receive practical accompaniment when needed.

DIDAR also creates spaces for cultural transmission, education, intergenerational exchange, arts, community participation and intercultural dialogue.

Community Support & Navigation

How DIDAR can help

Our role is to help people understand where to go, identify appropriate resources, communicate with institutions, connect with the right people and receive practical accompaniment when needed.

01

Navigation & Referral

Helping people understand available options and connect with appropriate community, administrative, educational, health, employment, legal or other specialized resources.

02

Practical Accompaniment

Accompanying people when practical support is needed to navigate appointments, institutions, administrative procedures or unfamiliar systems.

03

Communication & Documentation

Helping people organize information, prepare questions, document a situation, write clear communications and better understand institutional procedures.

04

Connection, Mentoring & Community

Connecting people with peers, volunteers, mentors, professionals, organizations and community networks while creating opportunities for participation and mutual support.

Our role: DIDAR provides community navigation, referral and accompaniment. It does not provide immigration, legal, medical, psychological or social-work advice, and it does not replace licensed or regulated professional services. When specialized expertise is needed, we help people identify and connect with appropriate qualified resources.

Who We Work With

A community open to different experiences and generations.

DIDAR works with people who may benefit from stronger community connections, practical guidance, access to resources or opportunities to participate and contribute.

DIDAR is open to the broader public, with a particular cultural and community focus on people connected to Iran, Afghanistan, Iraq, Syria, Pakistan, Central Asia, the Caucasus and neighbouring regions. We welcome intercultural participation and collaboration across communities.

Cross-cutting commitment

Sabz · سبز

Environment & Sustainable Community

DIDAR’s three pillars define its main areas of activity. Sabz is a cross-cutting environmental commitment that runs across DIDAR’s programs, projects and everyday operations rather than belonging to only one pillar.

It encourages practical environmental responsibility through reducing waste, reuse, repair, recycling, resource sharing and environmentally responsible choices.

Guiding Values

How we want to build community.

DIDAR’s activities may take different forms, but they are guided by a shared set of principles across programs, partnerships and community work.

Heritage & Continuity

Respect the histories, languages and memories that communities carry and create opportunities for them to be shared across generations.

Access & Inclusion

Create activities that are welcoming and accessible across age, income, language, ability and migration experience.

Learning & Creativity

Treat education, arts, curiosity and knowledge-sharing as tools for individual and community growth.

Dignity & Empowerment

Support people in strengthening autonomy, confidence and access to knowledge, resources and opportunities.

Intercultural Dialogue

Use culture as a bridge for exchange, mutual understanding and meaningful relationships between communities.

Reciprocity & Mutual Support

Foster a community in which people share knowledge, time, skills and resources, and where those who receive support are encouraged, when they are able, to pass that support forward to others.

Environmental Responsibility

Encourage reducing waste, reuse, recycling, repair, resource sharing and environmentally responsible choices throughout DIDAR’s activities.

Collaboration

Build partnerships with libraries, cultural organizations, universities, schools, professionals, businesses, environmental initiatives and community organizations.

Respect & Pluralism

Create spaces where different cultures, generations, identities and perspectives can participate with dignity and mutual respect.
