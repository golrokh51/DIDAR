About DIDAR

Community support with a cultural and intercultural approach

DIDAR develops community-based initiatives that strengthen autonomy, inclusion, social connection and participation. Our work includes service navigation, practical accompaniment, intercultural mediation, education, community activities, cultural programming and documentation of community needs.

We work especially with newcomers, international students, seniors, families, women and other people who may face linguistic, cultural, administrative or social barriers.

Services

How DIDAR can help

Newcomer & settlement support

Orientation to Québec systems, community resources, public services and everyday life.

Service navigation

Help identifying the right community, administrative, legal, health or institutional resources.

Digital inclusion

Support with smartphones, email, online forms, appointment portals and digital public services.

Senior support & accompaniment

Practical support for older adults, including digital assistance and accompaniment to medical or administrative appointments.

Intercultural & language mediation

Help understanding institutions, communication norms and cultural differences, with linguistic support when appropriate.

Students & researchers

Academic integration, institutional navigation, mentoring and support for international students and researchers.

Employment & professional integration

CVs, professional communication, networking, job-search orientation and workplace resource navigation.

Rights & institutional communication

Support documenting concerns, preparing questions or correspondence, and finding appropriate formal resources.

Community connection

Gatherings, peer support, volunteering, mentoring and activities that reduce isolation and build social networks.

Women & family support

Resource navigation and practical community support for women and families during vulnerable or transitional periods.

Culture, arts & education

Literature, educational activities, cultural events, intercultural exchange and transmission of cultural knowledge.

Community needs & advocacy

Documenting recurring barriers and communicating community needs to institutions and partner organizations.

DIDAR provides community navigation and accompaniment. It does not replace licensed medical, legal, psychological or other regulated professional services.

Projects

Current and developing initiatives

Literature · Heritage · Community

Bibliothèque Nima

A community library rooted in decades of Persian literary and cultural activity. Nima is being developed as a space for literature, documentation, cultural memory, reading activities and intergenerational transmission.

Seniors · Digital inclusion · Accompaniment

DIDAR Aînés

A senior-support initiative offering a free weekly drop-in for digital and service-navigation help, with scheduled accompaniment for more complex administrative and medical appointments.

Inclusion · Dialogue · Learning

Intercultural workshops & community events

Educational and public activities on inclusion, diversity, consent, gender, belonging, migration experiences, rights, coexistence and intercultural understanding.

Research · Documentation · Community needs

Community documentation & advocacy

Collecting recurring community concerns, documenting barriers and building evidence that can support better services, partnerships and institutional responses.
