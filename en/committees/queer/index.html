DIDAR · Committee

Queer Committee

Connection, inclusion, dignity and community-led dialogue.

← All committees

What this committee can explore

A space for queer community members and allies to shape ideas for connection, creativity and mutual support in ways that respect their privacy and agency.

Community connection

Create opportunities to exchange experiences and build supportive networks.

Learning and dialogue

Explore topics of inclusion, boundaries, respectful communication and solidarity.

Creative expression

Collaborate on artistic and cultural ideas where participants wish to contribute.

Safer participation

Discuss accessibility, voluntary participation, confidentiality and consent.

Scope and participation

Participation does not mean agreeing to be named, photographed or publicly identified. No member list is published without explicit consent.

Interested in taking part?

Contact DIDAR to ask about participation or propose an idea. Committee membership and public recognition are always subject to individual permission.
