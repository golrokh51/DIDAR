DIDAR · Committee

Cultural Heritage & Exchange Committee

Celebrating cultural traditions, heritage languages and the arts.

← All committees

What this committee can explore

A place to share cultural practices across generations and communities—not as fixed traditions, but as living knowledge that people can learn from together.

Festivals and gatherings

Explore possible celebrations and educational activities around Nowruz, Yalda and other cultural occasions.

Heritage languages

Create possibilities for language exchange and learning, including Turkish and Kurdish, with attention to the particular language variety and its speakers.

Traditional arts

Share crafts, music, oral histories, storytelling and other forms of cultural expression with appropriate attribution.

Intercultural dialogue

Invite people from different backgrounds to discover one another’s cultures without treating any community as a single voice.

Scope and participation

Nowruz and Yalda activities, Turkish and Kurdish classes, and traditional-art workshops are proposed areas of interest—not confirmed events or currently advertised courses.

Interested in taking part?

Contact DIDAR to ask about participation or propose an idea. Committee membership and public recognition are always subject to individual permission.
