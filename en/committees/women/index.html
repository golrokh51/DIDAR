What this committee can explore

This committee brings attention to women’s experiences, dialogue, learning and mutual support. The committee’s activities will be shaped by the people participating.

Voices and experiences

Create room for dialogue and knowledge-sharing on matters participants identify as important.

Learning together

Exchange practical skills, resources and perspectives.

Arts and culture

Explore storytelling, cultural expression and creative collaboration.

Community networks

Connect people with peer networks and appropriate community services.

Scope and participation

Participation guidelines and any future events should be agreed with committee members before publication.

Interested in taking part?

Contact DIDAR to ask about participation or propose an idea. Committee membership and public recognition are always subject to individual permission.
