## The DIDAR Charter

### Preamble

DIDAR was founded on the belief that human connection, education, culture, and collective participation can help build a more welcoming, informed, and cohesive society.

This charter sets out the shared principles and commitments that guide DIDAR's activities, collaborations, and interpersonal relationships. These principles apply to the coordinating team, volunteers, partners, and participants, in accordance with their respective roles and responsibilities.

### Article 1 — Respect for Human Dignity and Equality

At DIDAR, every person has the right to be welcomed with respect and without discrimination. We oppose racism, sexism, xenophobia, homophobia, transphobia, ableism, and all other forms of discrimination and social exclusion.

### Article 2 — A Respectful Environment Free from Harassment

We do not tolerate any form of harassment, threats, intimidation, violence, humiliation, or abuse of power. We strive to prevent such behaviour, listen to concerns, and address situations that arise responsibly.

### Article 3 — Consent, Autonomy, and Privacy

Participation in activities and the sharing of personal experiences are voluntary. The publication or sharing of a person's name, image, recorded voice, or personal story must take place with their informed consent. We handle personal information carefully, in accordance with our commitments and applicable privacy protection requirements.

### Article 4 — Respect for Cultural Diversity and Freedom of Thought

At DIDAR, no culture, language, identity, or worldview is considered superior to another. We encourage respectful dialogue and recognize everyone's right to hold different beliefs or to have no religious beliefs. Disagreements must not lead to insults, discrimination, or the exclusion of others.

### Article 5 — Preservation of Heritage and Access to Knowledge and the Arts

We work to preserve and transmit cultural heritage, languages, literature, oral history, and intergenerational experiences. At the same time, we support creativity, new forms of experimentation, accessible education, and participation in artistic and scientific activities.

### Article 6 — Voluntary Participation and Mutual Aid

DIDAR is founded on voluntary participation and mutual cooperation. Everyone may contribute according to their abilities, interests, and personal circumstances. Receiving assistance or benefiting from activities does not create any obligation to reciprocate or provide assistance in return.

### Article 7 — Accessibility and Removing Barriers to Participation

We strive to identify and reduce linguistic, economic, social, cultural, and physical or cognitive barriers to participation, within the limits of our available resources. Whenever possible, activities should be designed with consideration for participants' diverse needs and circumstances.

### Article 8 — Political and Religious Independence

DIDAR is an independent, nonpartisan, and nonreligious organization, unaffiliated with any political party or religious institution. No DIDAR activity may be used as a means of partisan or religious promotion or recruitment.

This independence does not mean withdrawing from social issues or remaining silent in the face of injustice. Defending human dignity, equality, and human rights, and opposing discrimination are consistent with DIDAR's independent identity.

### Article 9 — Environmental Responsibility and Responsible Use of Resources

In planning and carrying out our activities, we consider reducing unnecessary consumption, reusing materials, recycling, conserving resources, and making environmentally responsible choices. We also promote a culture of sharing and sustainable use of resources and facilities.

### Article 10 — Transparency, Accountability, and Recognition of Our Limitations

We act transparently regarding our goals, activities, roles, resources, and limitations, and we welcome feedback and constructive criticism. DIDAR is not a substitute for specialized medical, psychological, legal, or immigration services. When needed, we refer individuals to appropriate resources and professionals.

### Article 11 — Responsible Collaboration and Maintaining Independence

DIDAR's collaborations must align with its mission and values. When choosing partners and supporters, we consider our independence in decision-making, mutual respect, transparency, and the interests of the community. No collaboration or financial support should be interpreted as an endorsement of all the views or activities of the other party.
